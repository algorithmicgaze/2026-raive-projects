# Fruit drama prompts

Style suffix for every prompt:

> 3D animated, Pixar style, glossy skin, expressive face, cinematic lighting,
> shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical, high detail

Rules from the reference format:
- Describe the character by appearance, not by name. "An elderly broccoli
  woman in an ivory silk robe with a pearl necklace", not "Brenda".
- One emotion per scene. State it.
- State the camera: medium shot, waist up, full body.
- State the setting and the light.

## Scene prompts (text-to-video, first frame reference)

1. Dramatic scene in a luxurious penthouse living room at night. An elderly
   broccoli woman in an ivory silk robe and pearl necklace stands pointing
   accusingly at a tall grape man in a tailored black suit. Both have intense
   angry expressions. Floor-to-ceiling windows show the city skyline.
2. A tall pineapple woman in an emerald evening gown stands in a grand marble
   hallway under a crystal chandelier, hand on her chest, mouth open in shock,
   eyes wide. Medium shot, waist up. Sunset light.
3. A red apple man in a black suit sits at a rooftop restaurant table at golden
   hour, looking at his phone with a guilty smile. A dark cherry woman in a red
   satin dress watches him across the candle, suspicious.
4. A stern banana woman in a beige cardigan slams a document on a marble
   kitchen island. A nervous lemon man in a grey suit raises both hands.
   Morning light. Medium shot.
5. A small red apple boy in a navy school blazer stands in a hospital corridor,
   tears forming, looking up. Cold fluorescent light. Rain on the window.
6. A dark cherry woman in a red satin dress stands alone at a mansion gate in
   the rain at night, mascara running, clutching a suitcase. Wet asphalt
   reflections. Full body.
7. A tall pineapple woman in an emerald gown turns slowly toward the camera in
   a penthouse at night, one tear rolling down, lips trembling. Close medium
   shot.
8. A purple grape man in a black suit leans back in a courtroom chair with a
   smirk, arms crossed. Wood panels, harsh overhead light.

## Motion prompts (image-to-video from a reference frame)

Keep motion small. One action per clip.

- She gasps, raises her hand to her chest, and takes one step back.
- He turns his head slowly toward the camera and narrows his eyes.
- She points accusingly, then lowers her arm and shakes her head.
- He looks down at his phone, smiles, then hides it under the table.
- She wipes a tear with the back of her hand and lifts her chin.
- He raises both hands in defense and steps backward.
- She crosses her arms and taps her foot, glaring.
- He leans forward, slams a fist on the table, then sits back.
- She covers her mouth with both hands, eyes wide.
- He adjusts his tie, looks left, then right, nervous.
